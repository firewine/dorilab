## Input readiness

A thermal correlation input package lists component dissipation power and the location of each heat source. Checking whether these inputs are present does not determine whether a product meets its temperature performance requirement.

## Human review

An input-readiness draft and an engineering performance decision have different scopes. A human records the scope of the draft review separately from formal verification closure.
